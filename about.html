About Us

More than photography.
A feeling that lasts.

At ROOH STUDIOS, we believe every photograph holds a story. From meaningful glances to beautiful celebrations, we preserve the emotions, details and memories that deserve to last forever.

Our Story

Rooted in Lahore.

ROOH, meaning “soul”, is at the heart of everything we do. We are a Pakistani wedding photography studio drawn to the warmth, colour and feeling of Pakistani celebrations. Our work is image-led and story-first: we document weddings the way they are lived, then shape them into something you will want to return to.

We work closely with each couple to understand what matters most, so the photographs feel like you, not a template.

Our Philosophy

What we care about.

Natural Emotion
